This policy explains how {{ biz }} handles personal data in IP Evidence Hub.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email address and a hashed password.
  • Files you upload and their metadata (title, description, filename, size, SHA-256 fingerprint, timestamps).
  • Activity records: logins, uploads, downloads, reviews and payments, with time and IP address for security.
  • Payment records: amount, status and the Razorpay payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay and never reach our servers.

Why we use it

To provide and secure the service, verify evidence, process payments, and meet legal obligations.

What is public

The public verification and hash lookup pages show an evidence record's title, filename, size, fingerprint and timestamps. Fingerprints anchored on the MST blockchain are public and permanent. Do not put personal information in titles or filenames.

Sharing

We share data only with Razorpay (payments) and our hosting provider, and when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and as required by law afterwards.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data (except permanent blockchain records) by emailing {{ email }}.
